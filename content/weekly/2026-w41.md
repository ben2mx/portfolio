**October 5 to 11, 2026**

## What shipped

- **Found the real cause of a mailbox that kept filling up.** It had been treated as corruption. It was a retention setting. Fixed it the same night, then turned the finding into a phased data-governance rollout plan.
- **Every production virtual machine now says what it is for** inside the virtualization console, so nobody has to guess during an outage.
- **Delivered compliance audit evidence on request**, the same week it was asked for.

## What it means

**For the company:** email is kept for as long as the retention schedule says, not forever, and the virtualization console tells you what each production server is for.

**For the IT team:** work gets handed over with step-by-step instructions instead of one-line titles, so teammates can pick it up without a meeting.
